# Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of 100,000+ real orders from Olist, 
Brazil's largest e-commerce platform (2016-2018)
using Python and SQL.

## Business Questions
- What is the total revenue and growth trend?
- Which product categories drive most revenue?
- How does delivery performance vary by state?
- Does late delivery affect customer ratings?
- Who are the most valuable customers?

## Key Findings
- $15.4M total revenue over 2 years
- 10x revenue growth through 2017
- Late deliveries score 40% lower (2.57 vs 4.29 stars)
- 24% of customers are at risk of leaving
- Can't Lose Them segment could recover $2M if re-engaged

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
- SQL (SQLite)
- Jupyter Notebook
